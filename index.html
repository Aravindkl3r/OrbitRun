Features

Designed for the way testers actually work

Drop in any Excel file
Works with whatever format your team already uses. Smart column auto-detection maps your headers automatically — no reformatting needed.
One-click Pass / Fail / Skip
Mark results directly from the table or from the detail panel. After marking, the next pending test opens automatically — keep the momentum going.
Live execution dashboard
Stats cards, an animated progress bar, and a per-module sidebar update in real time as you work. Always know where you stand.
Steps & notes panel
Click any test case to see its full steps, expected result, and preconditions. Add bug IDs or notes alongside — all in one view.
Filter & search
Filter by status (Pending / Pass / Fail / Skip), switch between test suites, or search across all test cases instantly.
Export ready reports
Download an Excel report with three sheets: summary, full results, and failed cases only. Or export CSV for any other tool.
How it works

From spreadsheet to
signed-off in minutes

01
Import your Excel
Drag your .xlsx file onto the app. We detect columns automatically — ID, Title, Module, Priority, Steps, Expected Result.
02
Map the columns
A quick mapper lets you confirm or adjust which column goes where. Takes about 10 seconds.
03
Execute & mark
Work through tests one by one. Click Pass, Fail, or Skip. Add notes or bug IDs in the side panel.
04
Export the report
When done, download a complete Excel or CSV report ready to share with your team or attach to a ticket.
